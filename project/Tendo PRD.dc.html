1. Product overview

Tendo is a mobile app (iOS and Android) for keeping recurring personal commitments. Users define the things that make up a good day for them — making the bed, working out, studying, reading, brushing their teeth — and hold themselves to them every day. Tendo turns each day into a single 0–100% Discipline Score, tracks streaks, and lets users join small private groups where shared commitments are scored and ranked fairly.

Tendo is deliberately not a task manager. A task manager asks "what do I need to get done?"; Tendo asks "what did I commit to doing consistently?". The core loop is:

make commitments → keep them → measure consistency

The social layer extends it to keep your way, together. It is private, invitation-based, and has no feed, posts, comments, followers or messaging.

2. Problem statement

  • Habit trackers count check-marks. Anyone can reach a high number by adding trivial items, so the number means nothing.
  • To-do apps treat recurring commitments like one-off tasks, bury them under project work, and don't measure consistency.
  • Real life breaks rigid systems. Illness, travel and late nights break streaks unfairly, so people either give up or stop being honest with the app.
  • Accountability apps drift into social media: feeds, likes and public posts replace the actual commitment.
  • Users can't see why they got a score, so they stop trusting it.

3. Product vision

An honest, calm record of whether you kept your word to yourself. Tendo should feel supportive but not easy to game, flexible but still disciplined, social but not social media, and powerful but still minimal. It adjusts to real life (skips, reschedules, travel, recovery) while keeping an honest history of what happened.

Guiding rule for the mood and tone features: mood changes the conversation, not the rules.

4. Target users

  • The self-improver. 18–35. Wants stronger daily discipline around fitness, study, focus and sleep.
  • The everyday organiser. Wants the small things in a normal day to happen: chores, hygiene, tidying, hydration.
  • The accountability seeker. Keeps commitments better when 2–12 friends can see a score. Doesn't want a social network.
  • The irregular-schedule user. Night owls, shift workers and travellers, who need a custom day end, timezone safety and travel mode.

Decision required Minimum age and whether an age gate is needed at signup. The prototype collects no age.

5. Core value proposition

  • One honest number. A weighted daily score where the commitments you avoid count for more, with every point explained.
  • Real life is allowed. Skip, move, pause, travel, recovery and optional days are all recorded honestly, never silently.
  • Fair, private accountability. Groups compete on shared commitments by percentage, never on volume, and never see private commitments.
  • Genuinely free. Everything except more than two groups is free.

10. Navigation structure

Bottom tab bar, four tabs: Today · Groups · Stats · You. Tabs appear on top-level screens and their direct settings pages. Creation flows, detail screens, onboarding, check-in and full-screen timers hide the tab bar.

  • Today → Score breakdown · Commitment detail → Custom schedule · Add commitment → Packs → Pack · Manage commitments · Weekly Review · sheets (actions, move, log, focus, adjust, close-out/reflection, activity, link completion).
  • Groups → Group home → Group settings (member sheet, confirm delete) · Shared commitment create · Challenge · Create group · Join sheet · Friends → Friend profile · Upgrade sheet.
  • Stats → History (edit day).
  • You → Friends · Groups · Plan/Pro · Subscription · Downgrade · Day end sheet · Reminders · Notifications · History · Widgets · Calendar · Appearance · Tone · Recovery sheet · Travel mode · Weekly Review · Commitments · Privacy & Data (Social privacy, Notifications & email, Blocked users, Export, What Tendo stores, Privacy Policy, Terms, Delete account) · About Tendo (Licenses) · Preview close-out · Replay first run · Sign out.

Back behaviour. Every pushed screen has a text back link in the top-left naming its parent ("‹ Today", "‹ Groups", "‹ You"). Legal screens return to whichever screen opened them. Bottom sheets close on scrim tap and have an explicit Cancel or Not now wherever a choice is involved.

Decision required The Stats tab is labelled "Stats" in the tab bar, but the History back link reads "‹ Progress". Pick one name.

11. User journeys

J1 · New user to first completed day

  1. Welcome → Create an account → Register (name, email, password) → Verify email (6-digit code).
  2. Onboarding step 1: "What are you trying to build?" (free text or a chip).
  3. Step 2: "Start with these." — 5 balanced suggestions, toggle off any that don't fit.
  4. Step 3: "When does your day end?" (Midnight / 1 AM / 2 AM / 3 AM).
  5. Notification permission explainer → Continue triggers the OS prompt, or Not now.
  6. Mood check-in → Today's Priority (optional) → Today.
  7. Hold each commitment to complete it. The score shows "Too new to rate" until day 5.
  8. Close out the day → reflection if unfinished, otherwise the day summary.

J2 · Returning user, daily

Sign in (or stay signed in) → check-in (mood, priority, or Skip) → Today → optional "Anything different today?" to move, skip or mark items optional → complete through the day → close out.

J3 · Social

You or Groups → Friends → search a username → Add → friend accepts → Create group (name, goal, invite friends or share a code) → add shared commitments → compete on the weekly leaderboard → nudge a slipping member → Sunday group recap.

J4 · Hitting the Free limit

Free user in two groups taps Create group or Join with code → upgrade sheet "Keep going with Tendo Pro" → Upgrade to Pro (Pro screen) or Not now (dismiss, nothing lost).

J5 · Real life

Travelling next week → You → Travel mode → choose dates → tick commitments to pause → Start. Ill today → You → Recovery days → Use Recovery Day. Can't work out today → ⋯ → Move today → Tomorrow.

J6 · Leaving Pro

Subscription → Cancel Pro → Pro stays until the period ends → Downgrade screen: pick 2 groups to keep active → the rest become Inactive with history intact.

12. Commitment creation & management

Purpose. Let users capture anything that belongs to their day in a few seconds, and keep a tidy list as it grows.

User story. As a user, I want to add a commitment quickly — from a suggestion, a pack or my own words — and set how and when it repeats, so that Tendo tracks what actually matters to me.

Add commitment (screen order)

  1. Header: Cancel · Browse packs › · title "New commitment".
  2. Name field (free text, unrestricted).
  3. "How do you want to track this?" — Done / not done · Reach an amount · Spend time · Times per week. Non-simple types reveal a target stepper (section 13).
  4. Category: Body · Mind · Focus · Craft · Chores · Other (single select, wrapping pills).
  5. Suggested: 6 pills for the selected category + View more / Show less. Other shows no suggestions, only a line encouraging free text.
  6. Cadence: Every day · Weekdays · 3× a week · Custom.
  7. Why I do this (optional, one line).
  8. Weight card: "×1.0 — New commitments start at ×1.0…".
  9. Add commitment (disabled style until a name exists).

Business rules.

  • Tapping a suggestion fills the name field and selects that suggestion's recommended cadence. Both stay editable.
  • Recommended cadences: daily hygiene and chores → Every day; Laundry, Change bedsheets, Clean bathroom, Organise clothes → Custom; Workout, Run 5K, Mobility, Drawing, Side project → 3× a week; study and deep work → Weekdays.
  • New commitments start at weight ×1.0, streak 0, and appear on Today immediately.
  • Commitments added from Today go to the end of their time-of-day group.
  • Suggestion library: Body 11, Mind 8, Focus 8, Craft 8, Chores 16, Other 0.

Custom schedule

Opened from Commitment detail → Schedule (and from Weekly Review → Adjust schedule). Pattern: Every day · Weekdays · Weekends · Specific days · X times per week · Every X days. Specific days reveals a 7-day picker (M T W T F S S). Time of day: Morning · Afternoon · Evening · Any time. Starts (default today) and Ends (default no end). A plain-language summary ("Due Mon, Wed, Fri · afternoon, starting today") sits above Save schedule.

Decision required Input controls for "X times per week" count, "Every X days" interval, and start/end date pickers. The prototype shows static values.
Decision required Whether time of day is chosen on the Add screen or only in Custom schedule. The prototype only has it in Custom schedule, so new commitments default to "Any time".
Decision required Edit name, category and type after creation. The actions sheet has no Edit row; detail exposes only Schedule.

History snapshots. Editing a schedule never rewrites past days. Each day stores the schedule that applied on that date.

Commitment detail

Name, category · cadence, paused banner when relevant, tiles (Weight, Streak, Kept), tiles (Best streak, This month x/y), Momentum card (30-day % large, plus 7 days / 30 days / All time), Why I do this quote when set, "Why ×N?" explanation, last-14-days grid with a Kept / Missed / Skipped legend, Complete now / Completed today, More (actions sheet), Schedule row.

Manage commitments

From Today → Manage, or You → Commitments. Search field, status filter (Active · Paused · Archived), category filter (All + 6), count label, rows with a status badge and ⋯ actions. Empty result: "Nothing matches that. Try another filter." Footnote: "Archiving keeps the history and the analytics. Deleting doesn't."

Actions sheet (⋯)

Complete / Mark not done · Skip today · Pause / Resume · Archive / Restore · Optional today / Make it required · Make it today's priority / Remove priority · Start focus · Move today · Delete (destructive, red, last).

Edge cases. A duplicate name within the user's own list is allowed from the Add screen (only packs check duplicates) — Decision required whether to warn. Deleting skips confirmation in the prototype — Decision required. The spec asks for confirmation before permanent deletion; recommend a confirm sheet. The "Keep your list meaningful" nudge for many trivial commitments is not designed — Decision required.

Acceptance criteria.

  • A user can create a commitment with only a name. Every other field has a default.
  • Tapping a suggestion fills the name and selects its cadence. Both can be changed before saving.
  • Choosing Other shows no suggestion pills.
  • A new commitment appears on Today with ×1.0 and is included in the score from the moment it's added.
  • Changing a schedule does not change any past day in History.
  • Manage search matches name substrings, case-insensitive, combined with both filters.

13. Commitment types

Purpose. Not everything is yes/no. Tendo must measure amounts, time and weekly frequency without becoming a spreadsheet.

User story. As a user, I want to track "2 L of water" or "study 60 min" as progress, not just done/not done, so partial effort is recorded honestly.

Type (UI label)Target inputToday rowLoggingScore credit
Simple · Done / not done—Checkbox, name, metaHold to complete0 or 1
Amount · Reach an amountDaily target, stepper ±5"1.4 / 2 L" + progress barTap progress → Log sheet: +0.2 / +0.5 / +1 / Donevalue ÷ target, capped at 1
Duration · Spend timeMinutes/day, stepper ±15"42 / 60 min" + barLog sheet +15 / +30 / +60 / Done, or Start a timer insteadminutes ÷ target, capped at 1
Weekly target · Times per weekTimes/week, stepper ±1"2 / 3 this week" + barLog sheet +1 / DoneSee rule below

Business rules.

  • The type picker reuses the Add screen. There are no separate creation flows.
  • Logging never exceeds the target.
  • Partial credit is always shown in words on the Log sheet ("67% of this commitment right now") and on the score breakdown.
  • Duration never forces a timer. Manual logging is always available.
  • Weekly targets have no fixed weekday. They count as complete once the target is reached, whenever that happens.

Decision required How a weekly target affects the daily score. The prototype credits progress ÷ target each day (2/3 → 67% of that item's points every day this week), while the Log sheet copy says weekly targets "score on the week, not the day". Options: (a) exclude from daily score and count only in the weekly average; (b) count only on days a session is logged; (c) current proportional behaviour.
Decision required Unit selection for Amount (L, steps, pages, glasses). The prototype hard-codes units per sample commitment and has no unit field.
Decision required Whether proportional credit can be switched off per commitment ("if enabled" in the brief).

Edge cases. Lowering a target below today's logged value: cap display at target, keep the raw value. Focus timer finishing on a duration commitment: Decision required whether elapsed time auto-logs. The prototype does not auto-log.

Screens. Add commitment, Today rows, Log sheet, Focus timer, Score breakdown.

Acceptance criteria. A 40/60 min duration shows "40 / 60 min", a two-thirds bar, and "27 / 40" points (for a 40-point item) in the breakdown. Tapping the progress label opens the Log sheet without completing or opening detail. Done sets value = target and marks the item complete.

15. Discipline Score

Purpose. A single, objective, explainable measure of how well you kept today's personal commitments.

User story. As a user, I want one percentage for my day and to see exactly how it was calculated, so I trust it.

Calculation (as prototyped)

weight(c) = round1( 1 + (1 − completionRate30d(c)) × 1.6 ) // ≥ 1.0
points(c) = round( weight(c) × 10 )
credit(c) = 1 if completed; value/target (≤1) for amount, duration, weekly; else 0
due = personal commitments, pending or completed, NOT optional, NOT paused,
              NOT archived, NOT travel-paused, NOT moved away today, NOT skipped
score = round( Σ points(c)·credit(c) ÷ Σ points(c) × 100 ) over due

Business rules.

  • Personal only. Shared/group commitments never enter the personal score. They feed that group's score.
  • Adaptive weight. Commitments you avoid gain weight. Weight is always shown openly on every row (×N) and explained on detail ("Why ×1.9?").
  • Mood never affects the score, weights, requirements or leaderboards.
  • Today's Priority is visual only, with no extra points.
  • Too new to rate: the score shows "—" with "Too new to rate · day N of 5" until 5 days of history exist.
  • Comparison line: "±N vs your 30-day average".
  • Score updates immediately on every completion, undo, log or state change.

Score breakdown screen

Opened by tapping the gauge ("SEE THE MATH"). Shows the score and delta, a one-line explanation, then THE MATH: each due commitment with state (Completed / Missed / "1.4 of 2 L — 70%") and earned/possible points, skipped items as "Skipped — excluded, — / —", a total "Earned / available" row, the skip rule note, an Adaptive Weight card naming the currently heaviest commitment, and Correct yesterday's entries.

Decision required Final weight formula, cap and window. The prototype uses the formula above with a 30-day window and no upper cap (max ×2.6). The design copy describes the heaviest example as ×1.9. Recommend a cap of ×2.0.
Decision required Weight update frequency (nightly at day close is recommended).
Decision required "Successful day" threshold used by Weekly Review ("clean days") and streaks. The onboarding step asking "What counts as a successful day? 70/80/90/100%" was specified but is not in the prototype.
Decision required Score when every due item is skipped or optional (0 due). Recommend "—" plus "Nothing due today".

Acceptance criteria. The sum of breakdown rows equals the total row. The total row ratio equals the displayed percentage. Skipping an item changes the denominator and never lowers the score. Adding a group commitment never changes the personal score.

16. Streaks

Purpose. Reward unbroken consistency per commitment, per user and per group.

  • Commitment streak. Consecutive scheduled occurrences completed. Shown on the Today meta line, detail tiles (Streak, Best streak) and Weekly Review ("Best streak").
  • Personal streak. Shown on the You profile (STREAK, BEST), friend rows, widgets and the History summary. Recovery days preserve it; travel-paused items don't break it.
  • Group streak. Consecutive days on which every member completed the shared commitments. "It breaks the moment one person misses."

Decision required What keeps the personal streak alive: 100% day, threshold day, or at least one completion.
Decision required Whether a skip breaks, pauses or continues a commitment streak.
Decision required Whether optional or skipped shared items break the group streak.
Decision required How weekly-target and every-X-days commitments count streaks (per week / per occurrence).

Missed-day state. On the morning after a broken streak, Today shows "Yesterday closed at 34%. Your 9-day streak reset. The score recovers in about four clean days." Neutral, factual, no guilt.

Acceptance criteria. Streaks never break because of a timezone change, a Recovery day or travel-paused items. Users can hide their streak from others (Privacy).

17. Mood check-in & adaptive tone

Purpose. Make Tendo feel human on hard days without making the score subjective.

User story. As a user, I want to tell Tendo how I'm feeling in one tap so it speaks to me appropriately, without lowering the bar.

Check-in flow

  1. "How are you feeling today?" — Rough · Low · Okay · Good · Great (single select). Continue is enabled once one is chosen. Skip is always visible. Footnote: "Only you can see this. Never shared with friends or groups."
  2. "What matters most today?" — up to 5 of today's commitments, pick one. Start the day or No priority today.

Shown on the first open of each Tendo day, after sign-in and after onboarding.

Tone

You → Tendo Tone: Gentle · Balanced (default) · Push me. Mood shifts the effective tone by one step (Rough/Low −1, Good/Great +1), producing a gentle, balanced or direct lean. The lean changes only the coach line under the score and similar copy, for example:

  • Gentle: "Start with one. That is enough to begin." / "One left. No rush."
  • Balanced: "Three left. Still winnable." / "One left. Finish it."
  • Direct: "Three left. You know the plan."

Rules. Mood never changes weights, score, requirements, commitments or leaderboards. Mood is private: never on profiles, groups, leaderboards or friends. Copy never uses guilt or shame. A Push-me user on a low day still gets direct copy, one step softer.

Decision required Whether mood can be changed later in the day, and whether skipping check-in suppresses it until tomorrow.
Decision required The full copy matrix for all tone/lean states beyond the coach line.

Screens. Check-in (mood), Check-in (priority), Today (Priority card, coach line), You (Tendo Tone).

Acceptance criteria. The score is identical for the same completions regardless of mood. Skip reaches Today with no priority set. The priority item is removed from the main list and shown in the Today's Priority card, with Focus when eligible.

18. Weekly Review

Purpose. A short weekly moment to look back and adjust — a signature screen, not a report.

User story. As a user, at the end of each week I want to see how I did in under a minute and decide whether to change anything.

Behaviour. Header "WEEK OF 14–20 SEPTEMBER" · "Your week" · average Discipline Score (large) · delta vs last week · tiles: Clean days, Best streak · Hardest and Strongest commitment with % · See the detail expands daily bars (Mon–Sun %) and commitment consistency bars · "Anything you want to change next week?" · Keep everything (primary) / Review commitments (→ Manage) / Adjust schedule (→ Custom schedule).

Entry points. Today banner "Your week is ready." (until opened) · You → Weekly review (Sundays) · Weekly Review notification.

Rules. Uses only existing Tendo data; no AI. Group weekly recap is separate (section 23).

Decision required Week-ending day setting (Sunday default; user-configurable?).
Decision required Definition of "clean day" (see section 15).
Decision required Whether past weekly reviews are browsable.

Edge cases. Fewer than 7 days of history: show available days, hide the vs-last-week delta. Recovery and travel days are shown in the daily list but excluded from the average — Decision required.

Acceptance criteria. The average equals the mean of the displayed daily scores. The Today banner disappears once the review is opened.

19. Commitment Packs

Purpose. Curated, editable starting points so users can set up a whole routine at once. Not a marketplace.

User story. As a new user, I want to add a "Morning Reset" in one tap and remove what doesn't fit.

Browse. From Add commitment → Browse packs ›. Search (matches pack names and the commitments inside them) · category chips: All, Daily Life, Fitness, Study, Focus, Mind, Home, Sleep, Self-care, Work, Creativity, Travel · minimal cards "Name / N commitments · Category". No illustrations.

Library (32). Morning Reset, Night Reset, Better Sleep, Gym Routine, Beginner Fitness, Running, Exam Week, University Day, Deep Work, Productive Workday, Coding Routine, Reading Habit, Clean Room, Weekly Cleaning, Personal Hygiene, Self-Care, Mental Reset, Phone Detox, Better Eating, Hydration, Creative Routine, Writing Routine, Language Learning, Side Project, Weekend Reset, Travel Routine, Minimal Day, Discipline Starter, No-Procrastination, Work From Home, Posture & Mobility, Digital Clean-Up. Each item carries a default cadence and time of day.

Preview & customise. Pack screen lists included items with cadence · time. Customize makes rows toggleable. CTA "Add N commitments" reflects the real count after duplicates.

Duplicate handling. Items matching an existing commitment name (case-insensitive) show "ALREADY HAVE". A card offers Keep existing (default) · Add another · Skip duplicate. In customise mode duplicates start unticked. Never silently duplicate.

Rules. Added items become normal commitments (editable, pausable, archivable) with ×1.0, category = pack category. Packs are free. No paid, creator or AI packs.

Decision required Pack categories (Daily Life, Home, etc.) differ from commitment categories (Body, Mind, Focus, Craft, Chores, Other). Define the mapping when a pack item is added. The prototype writes the pack category, which is not one of the six.
Decision required A unified search across suggestions, packs and categories was specified. The prototype searches packs only.
Decision required "Keep existing" and "Skip duplicate" behave identically in the prototype. Define the difference or merge them.

Acceptance criteria. Adding Morning Reset to an account that already has "Make bed" adds 5, not 6, by default. The CTA count always matches what is actually added. Nothing is added if every selected item is a kept duplicate; the user sees "Nothing new to add".

20. Friends & friend requests

Purpose. Private, mutual connections for accountability. No followers.

User story. As a user, I want to add people I know by username and see how they're doing, within what they allow.

Behaviour. Friends screen (from Groups and You): search by username → results with Add → "Requested" → "Friends" · Pending requests with Accept / Decline · friends list with weekly score and streak (or "streak hidden") · empty state "No one yet." Friend profile: initials avatar, display name, @username, optional status line, This week / Streak / Best, Groups in common, privacy note, Invite to a group, Remove.

Rules. Friendship is mutual and requires acceptance. Visibility follows the friend's privacy settings. No follower counts, posts or bios beyond one status line. Commitment names are never shown on profiles.

Decision required Block and Report from the friend profile. They exist on the group member sheet but not on the profile; the brief asks for them on profiles.
Decision required Username rules (uniqueness, allowed characters, where users set it). @handles appear but there is no username field at signup.
Decision required Where the status line is edited.

Acceptance criteria. Search returns only exact-or-prefix username/name matches, excluding blocked users. Accepting moves the person into the friends list and triggers a toast. Remove takes effect immediately and returns to Friends.

21. Groups

Purpose. Small private circles (up to 12 people) that keep each other accountable on shared commitments.

User story. As a user, I want to create a group with friends, set shared commitments, and see who's keeping up, without a social feed.

Groups list

Title, limit label ("2 / 2 groups" or "Pro · unlimited"), explainer, cards (name, group score %, member count · streak, "You're 3rd of 8", 7-day bars), Join with code, + Create group, footnote, Friends row. Empty: "Discipline holds better with witnesses."

Create group

Name · goal (optional) · first shared commitment · invite friends (checklist) · invite code (6 characters, expires in 48 h) · Create group (enabled once named).

Join

23. Group scores & leaderboards

Purpose. Fair competition that rewards consistency, not volume.

  • Group score = completion across that group's shared commitments this week. Never includes private commitments.
  • Member score on the leaderboard = the member's percentage on the group's shared commitments.
  • Periods: Today · This Week (default, 7-day average) · This Month (30-day average).
  • Ranking by percentage only. Never by task count, private commitments or XP. Ties share a rank (e.g. 1, 1, 3). No tie-breakers.
  • Your row is highlighted. Members below the slipping threshold show Nudge (if nudges are allowed).
  • Footnote under the leaderboard: "Ranked on [period] — percentage, not task count. Equal scores share a place."

Nudges

Nudge → sheet with 4 presets only ("Checking in.", "You're two away.", "Don't break the chain.", "Strong week. Keep going."). No free text. One nudge per person per day; the button becomes "Sent". Recipients control nudges globally (Everyone / Friends only / Groups only / Off), per group, and per person (member sheet → Disable nudges from X).

Weekly recap (in group)

Group score with delta vs last week, "3 of 5 members improved. Top group streak: 12 days.", Strongest shared commitment, Most room to improve. Collaborative wording only; never names the lowest-ranked member.

Decision required The slipping threshold for showing Nudge (the prototype uses < 70% for the period).
Decision required How a member who hides their score appears on the leaderboard.
Decision required Treatment of new members with fewer than 7 days in "This Week".

Acceptance criteria. Two members at 94% both show rank 1 and the next shows rank 3. Adding private commitments never changes a group rank. A second nudge to the same person on the same day is impossible.

27. Two-group Free limit

Rule. A Free user can create or belong to at most 2 active groups, counting both created and joined.

  • Tapping Create group or Join with code at 2/2 opens the upgrade sheet instead.
  • Pro is never advertised elsewhere unprompted (only the You → Plan card, which is always present).

Decision required Accepting a group invite from a notification at 2/2 (same upgrade sheet is recommended).
Decision required Whether inactive groups (after downgrade) count toward the limit (recommended: no).

Acceptance criteria. A Free user can never end up in 3 active groups by any path. Leaving a group at 2/2 immediately re-enables Create and Join.

31. One-day rescheduling

Purpose. Move a single occurrence without touching the recurring schedule.

User story. As a user, when I can't work out this Wednesday, I want to move just today's workout to tomorrow.

Options: Tomorrow (Thu date) · Later this week (Sat date) · Choose a date · Cancel. If the target day is heavy: "You already have 9 commitments tomorrow. You can still move it." Never blocked. Toast confirms "…moved to tomorrow. Schedule unchanged."

Rules. The moved occurrence leaves today's list and score and is added to the target day.

Decision required Choose-a-date picker. Whether the target day already having that commitment creates two occurrences. Streak treatment. Whether moving is allowed after the day ends. The conflict warning threshold (the prototype always shows it).

Acceptance criteria. After moving, the commitment's schedule in detail is unchanged. It's absent from today and present on the chosen day. Today's score recalculates without it.

33. Accessibility

  • Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body and secondary text in both themes. Muted text #9A8E82 on #14100D is the lowest permitted.
  • Touch targets ≥ 44 × 44 pt (row ⋯ buttons are 44 wide).
  • State is never conveyed by colour alone: ✓ box, dash, strikethrough and text badges (SKIPPED, PAUSED, OPTIONAL, ARCHIVED, TRAVEL, RECOVERY).
  • Screen-reader labels on every commitment row: name, state and action ("Hold to complete / undo"). Every ⋯ labelled "More actions for [name]". Toggles expose switch role and state.
  • Hold-to-complete needs an accessible alternative. Decision required Recommend a VoiceOver/TalkBack custom action "Complete" plus the detail screen's Complete now.
  • Dynamic type up to the largest sizes without truncation. No fixed-height text boxes.
  • Charts have text equivalents (numbers beside every bar; the Stats chart has a summary line).
  • Animations are non-essential; respect Reduce Motion (skip the sink-to-bottom animation and hold sweep).
  • Logical reading order matches visual order. Sheets trap focus and announce as dialogs.

37. Acceptance criteria summary

Authentication & onboarding

  • Register validates name, email format and password ≥ 8, with inline errors and a 3-level strength meter. Show/Hide toggles the password.
  • Verify accepts exactly 6 digits (non-digits stripped). Resend shows a toast. "Change it" returns to Register.
  • Sign in validates, shows "Signing in…", then goes to check-in. "Stay signed in" defaults on. Forgot sends a reset link and returns to Sign in.
  • Apple/Google on Register → onboarding; on Sign in → check-in.
  • Onboarding is 3 steps. Step 2 requires ≥ 1 selection ("Pick at least one"). Step 3 sets day end. Then the permission explainer, then check-in.
  • Decision required The specified onboarding additions (day start time, successful-day threshold, packs in onboarding) aren't designed.

Today

  • Holding a pending row fills a sweep for a duration proportional to its weight, then completes it. Releasing early cancels. A short tap opens detail. Holding a completed or skipped row undoes it.
  • Completed rows stay in place ~0.5 s, then animate below all unfinished rows within their time-of-day group. Undone rows return to their original position. Unfinished order is never changed.
  • Time-of-day sections (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Any time, each with a done/total count) appear only when more than 4 items are due and they span more than 1 section.
  • Group-shared rows show the group badge. Measured rows show progress, a bar and a tappable progress label.
  • The score gauge, number and coach line update on every change without reload.
