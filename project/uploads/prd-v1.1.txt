1. Product overview
Tendo is a mobile app for recurring personal commitments. Users define the things that make up a good day for them - making
the bed, working out, studying, reading, brushing their teeth - and hold themselves to those commitments consistently. Tendo
turns each day into a single 0-100% Discipline Score, tracks streaks and progress, and supports small private groups where
shared commitments are scored and ranked fairly.
Tendo is not a task manager
A task manager asks: "What do I need to get done?" Tendo asks: "What did I commit to doing consistently?"
Core loop: make commitments -> keep them -> measure consistency.
Social layer: keep your way, together. Groups are private and invitation-based. There is no public feed, posts, comments,
follower system or direct messaging in the MVP.
2. Problem statement
 Habit trackers can reward trivial check-marks, making a headline number easy to game and hard to trust.
 To-do apps treat recurring commitments like one-off tasks and usually do not measure consistency over time.
 Real life breaks rigid systems. Illness, travel, shift work and late nights can unfairly destroy streaks.
 Accountability products often drift into social-media behaviour instead of helping people keep commitments.
 Users lose trust when they cannot understand exactly why a score changed.
3. Product vision
Tendo is an honest, calm record of whether you kept your word to yourself. It should feel supportive but not easy to game,
flexible but still disciplined, social but not social media, and powerful without becoming busy.
Guiding rule
Mood changes the conversation, not the rules. Mood never changes score, weights, requirements or leaderboard
outcomes.
4. Target users
User Need
Self-improver Stronger daily discipline around fitness, study, focus and sleep.
Everyday organiser Consistency with chores, hygiene, tidying, hydration and other
ordinary routines.
Accountability seeker Private mutual accountability with a small group of friends, without a
social network.
Irregular-schedule user Custom day end, timezone safety, rescheduling, travel and recovery
handling.
Age rule
MVP is intended for users aged 16 and older. Signup uses a simple 16+ confirmation rather than collecting date of birth. If
legal review for launch markets requires a different mechanism, the age gate may change without altering core product
behaviour.

 One honest number. A weighted personal Discipline Score with a transparent breakdown.
 Real life is allowed. Skip, move, pause, travel, recovery and optional states are recorded explicitly.

Bottom navigation uses four tabs: Today · Groups · Progress · You. The previous "Stats" label is retired; all references use
"Progress".
 Today -> Score breakdown, Commitment detail, Add commitment, Packs, Manage commitments, Weekly Review and
contextual sheets.
 Groups -> Group home, settings, shared commitments, challenges, Create/Join, Friends and upgrade sheet.
 Progress -> 30-day Progress view -> History and recent-day correction.
 You -> Friends, Groups, Plan/Pro, Subscription, Day end, Reminders, Notifications, Appearance, Tone, Recovery, Travel,

Creation flows, onboarding, check-in, detail flows and full-screen focus timer hide the bottom tab bar. Pushed screens use a
text back link naming the parent. Choice sheets always provide an explicit Cancel or Not now.
11. User journeys
J1 - New user to first day
1. Create account -> verify email.
2. Choose username and confirm 16+ eligibility.
3. Complete three-step onboarding: goal, starter commitments, day-end time.
4. Notification explainer -> OS prompt only after Continue.
5. Mood check-in -> optional Today Priority -> Today.
6. Complete/log commitments; score remains Too new to rate until sufficient history exists.
7. Day auto-closes at configured day end; user may also close out manually.
J2 - Returning daily
Open -> mood check-in (or Skip) -> optional priority -> Today -> optionally adjust the day -> complete/log through the day ->
close-out/reflection.
J3 - Social accountability
Friends -> search username -> request/accept -> Create group -> invite -> shared commitment acceptance -> group
leaderboard -> rate-limited nudges -> weekly recap.
J4 - Free group limit
At 2 active groups, Create/Join/Accept invite opens the same calm Pro upgrade sheet. Dismissing it changes nothing.

15. Discipline Score
Purpose
A single objective, explainable measure of how well the user kept today's personal commitments.
Adaptive weight
Final formula
For commitments with at least 5 scheduled occurrences in the last 30 days: weight = 1 + miss rate, rounded to the nearest
0.1 and capped at x2.0. New/insufficient-history commitments remain x1.0. Weight updates once at day close.
30-day completion Weight example
100% x1.0
80% x1.2
50% x1.5
20% x1.8
0% x2.0

Daily score
Due personal commitments include pending/completed measurable commitments that are not optional, skipped, paused,
archived, travel-paused or moved away. Group commitments never enter the personal score. Weekly-target commitments are
excluded from the daily denominator.
For each due commitment, points = round(weight x 10). Credit is 1 for a completed Simple item or proportional value/target for
Amount and Duration. Daily Score = round(sum(points x credit) / sum(points) x 100).
 Mood never affects score or weight.
 Today Priority is visual only.
 Score updates immediately on completion, undo, logging or state changes.
 If nothing is due, show "- · Nothing due today". Do not show 100%.
 Until 5 completed Tendo days exist, show "Too new to rate" instead of a headline score comparison.
Successful day threshold
Default successful-day threshold is 80%. Users may choose 70%, 80%, 90% or 100% in Settings. This threshold drives the
personal daily streak and Weekly Review clean-day count.
Score breakdown
Tapping the score shows each due commitment, its state/progress, weight, earned/possible points, excluded skipped items,
the total, the skip rule and a concise explanation of the heaviest adaptive weight. Every displayed score must reconcile exactly
to the breakdown total.
16. Streaks
Streak Rule
Commitment streak
Consecutive scheduled occurrences completed. Skip preserves but
does not increment. Reschedule follows the moved occurrence.
Weekly-target streaks count successful weeks; Every-X-days counts
scheduled occurrences.
Personal streak
Increments when daily score meets the user's success threshold.
Recovery, Travel and naturally empty days preserve but do not
increment. A scored day below threshold resets.
Group streak
Consecutive group days where every participating member
completes 100% of required shared commitments due that day. A
normal Skip does not satisfy the streak. Group-authorized
optional/excused items are excluded.
Timezone changes never break streaks. The previous day remains anchored to its original timezone/day boundary.
17. Mood check-in & adaptive tone
First open each Tendo day: Rough · Low · Okay · Good · Great. Skip is always visible. Mood is private and is never shown to
friends, groups or leaderboards.
 Users may change today's mood once before day close from Today.
 Skipping suppresses the check-in until the next Tendo day.
 Tone preference: Gentle · Balanced (default) · Push me. Mood shifts copy by at most one step.
 Use a curated copy matrix, not generated text. Copy remains calm and never uses guilt or shame.
 Mood changes copy only; it never changes score, weight, requirements or ranking.
18. Weekly Review
Weekly Review is a short Sunday review, not an analytics report. Sunday is fixed for MVP; configurable week-ending day is
post-MVP.

20. Friends & friend requests
Friendships are mutual and require acceptance. Search works by display name or username. No follower model exists.
Username rules
Username is chosen during onboarding after account creation. It is 3-20 characters, case-insensitive unique, and may contain
letters, numbers and underscore. Users may change it once every 30 days.
Friend profile
Shows allowed weekly score/streak data, groups in common, Invite to group, Remove friend, Block and Report. Commitment
names are never shown on profiles. Optional status is limited to 60 characters and edited from Edit Profile.
21. Groups
Groups are private circles of up to 12 people. They exist for shared accountability, not social posting.

Ownership
An owner must transfer ownership before leaving. If the owner is the only member, the app offers Delete Group instead.
Custom group icons are deferred; MVP uses initials avatars. Rename lives in Group Settings.
Creation and capacity
 Group name required; goal optional; first shared commitment optional.
 Invite friends or share a 6-character code that expires after 48 hours.
 At 12 members, joining/inviting is blocked with clear copy.
 Challenges are preset templates in MVP; no custom challenge builder.
22. Shared commitments
Shared commitments belong to a group and each participant keeps them individually. They never enter the personal Discipline
Score.
 Creation always shows the audience label before save.
 Invited members must accept participation before the commitment appears on their Today screen.
 A shared-commitment detail screen shows schedule, participants and permitted completion status.
 Group scoring uses equal weights. Adaptive personal weights never apply to shared commitments.
 If a shared commitment matches a personal commitment, ask Count for both or Keep separate. Never merge silently.

23. Group scores & leaderboards
Group scores measure only that group's shared commitments. Ranking is by percentage, never by personal task count or XP.
Ties share the same rank with no artificial tie-breaker.
Period Rule
Today Shared commitments due today
This Week Default; average of scored group days
This Month 30-day group performance
New members and privacy
 New members are Unranked until they have 3 scored group days. Then they rank using available group days until they
reach the full period.
 Personal Discipline Score privacy never hides the member's group-specific shared score from that group.
 Nudge appears when a member has unfinished shared commitments due today, not from a generic low-percentage
threshold.
Nudges
Preset messages only; no free text. One nudge per recipient per sender per day. Queued nudges count toward the limit when
queued and expire at the recipient's Tendo day end.
Weekly recap
Shows group score and delta, number of members who improved, strongest shared commitment, most room to improve and
top group streak. Never names the lowest performer.

27. Two-group Free limit
 Free users may create or belong to at most 2 active groups.
 At 2/2, Create, Join and Accept invite all open the same Pro upgrade sheet.

28. Pro upgrade & downgrade behaviour
Upgrade
The natural upgrade moment is attempting a third active group. The sheet explains that Pro lifts the group limit while
everything else the user already uses remains free. Not now is always clearly visible. No fake urgency, timers or strike-through
pricing.
Downgrade
 Cancelling keeps Pro until the paid period ends.
 If more than 2 groups remain, start a 7-day grace period and ask the user to choose 2 active groups.
 If no choice is made by the deadline, keep the 2 most recently active groups; all others become inactive, not deleted.
 If the user owns a group that becomes inactive for them, transfer ownership to the oldest Admin; if none, to the longest-
tenured remaining member.
 Inactive groups keep history, stop shared commitments/leaderboard participation/notifications for that user, and can be
reactivated by upgrading or freeing a slot.

30. Travel, Recovery & flexible-day behaviour
Travel Mode
 Native From / Until date pickers; future trips may be scheduled.
 User chooses which active commitments pause while away.
 Paused items are excluded from score and do not break streaks.
 Travel automatically ends at the next Tendo-day boundary after the Until date.
 History clearly marks Travel days.
Recovery Day
 Allowance: 2 Recovery Days per calendar quarter.
 Can be applied only to the current day before it closes; not retroactive.
 No numeric Discipline Score is used for that day or included in averages.
 Personal streak is preserved but does not increment.
 Recovery is explicit in Today and History and is never triggered silently.
Heavy-day suggestion
When 8 or more required personal commitments are due, Tendo may offer a rule-based suggestion to mark some Optional. It
never changes commitments automatically.
Anything different today?
Accessible after morning check-in and from Today all day. The sheet scrolls through every due commitment and offers Move ·
Optional · Skip. It is never limited to the first five.
Day end and corrections
 Day-end choices: 11:00 PM, Midnight, 1:00 AM, 2:00 AM, 3:00 AM and Custom.
 The Tendo day auto-closes at the configured boundary if the user does nothing.
 Recent-day correction window is 12 hours after close. Corrections always show "Edited after day ended".
 Close-out reflection is optional and never affects score.
31. One-day rescheduling
 Move only today's occurrence; recurring schedule remains unchanged.
 Choose Tomorrow, Later this week or native date picker up to 30 days ahead.
 If the destination already has the same commitment occurrence, ask before creating another.
 Reschedule is not available after the source day closes; use Edit Day for correction instead.
 If target day already has 8+ required commitments, show a warning but never block the move.
 Moved occurrence is excluded from the source score and becomes due on the destination day.
32. Offline, timezone & sync
Offline-first personal actions
Viewing Today, completing/logging, cached history and creating/editing personal commitments all work offline and save locally
first. The UI shows a quiet Offline banner and syncs when connectivity returns.
Queued social actions
Friend requests may remain queued. Group-code joins respect the invite code's 48-hour expiry. Nudges count against the
daily limit when queued and expire at the recipient's Tendo-day end.
