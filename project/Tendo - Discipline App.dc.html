TURN 1 · TWO DIRECTIONS
Both are the same working app — nine screens, hold-to-complete, weighted score, group leaderboard. They differ in surface and type. Pick one and I'll take it the rest of the way.
1A
Warm dark
Charcoal with a warm cast, sage accent, soft filled cards. Reads calm and physical — closer to a notebook on a desk than a dashboard.
1B
Paper dark
Ink on warm off-black. No cards — hairline rules, square corners, larger serif, wider letterspacing on labels. Editorial and severe.
WHAT'S CLICKABLE
Four tabs at the bottom. Press and hold a task — the fill sweeps for as long as the task's weight demands, then the gauge moves. Tap a group to open its leaderboard; Nudge on a slipping member opens the preset sheet. You → Replay first run shows onboarding.
THE SCORE
Weighted, not counted. Weight is inferred from your own history — kept 41% of the time means ×1.9, kept 86% means ×1.2 — and shown openly on every row, because a hidden penalty feels arbitrary. Shared tasks are excluded; they feed a separate group contribution.
EDGE STATES
Open Tweaks on either phone to switch Home between normal, empty, all-done, too-new-to-rate, and missed-day — and to jump straight to any screen.
OPEN
Group creation, invite-by-code, and the nightly close-out notification aren't drawn yet. Say the word and they go in the direction you pick.
